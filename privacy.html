Privacy Policy

This page explains how the current Notepad website handles information when you use the application.

Last updated: August 21, 2026

1. Information stored by Notepad

The current application stores your working note, saved versions, editor preferences, and timer state in your browser's local storage. This information is stored on the device and browser you use to access the application.

Notepad does not currently operate a server-side account system or a server database for the contents of your notes.

2. Sharing and downloads

When you choose to use your browser's sharing feature, the content you choose to share is passed to the sharing mechanism provided by your browser and operating system. When you download or copy a note, that action is initiated by you.

3. Cookies and advertising

The current version of Notepad is prepared for possible future advertising but does not claim that advertising services are active on this version of the site.

If Google AdSense or another advertising service is introduced, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant cookies, local storage, advertising technology providers, and choices available to visitors. Where required, a consent mechanism will be provided before applicable personalized advertising is served.

4. Third-party services

The application may rely on browser-native features such as local storage, fullscreen, and web sharing. Third-party services may be introduced in the future for advertising, analytics, security, or other site functions. Any such services will be described in an updated privacy policy.

5. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the contact method listed on the Contact page.

6. Important note

This page is a website policy template prepared for the current product architecture. Before the site is submitted for advertising approval or launched commercially, the owner should review it and replace any missing business or contact details with accurate information.
