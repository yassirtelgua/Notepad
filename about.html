About TelNoto

A simple place to write, save, and organize ideas.

TelNoto is a browser-based writing workspace designed for quick notes, focused writing, and everyday text editing without requiring a desktop installation.

Built for everyday writing

TelNoto focuses on the part of writing that matters most: opening a page and getting words down. The workspace combines a clean editor with practical tools for formatting, copying, downloading, sharing, version history, reading, focus, and fullscreen writing.

The current version is designed around local browser storage. Your working note is saved in the browser on the device you are using, rather than being uploaded to a TelNoto server.

Privacy-first by design

TelNoto does not require an account to start writing. The application stores the current note and related editor settings in your browser's local storage. Features such as sharing or downloading are activated by your own actions.
